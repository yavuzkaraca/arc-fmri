\section*{2. Instructions}
Give the participant the \texttt{Task\_Instructions} handout matching their participant number and go through it together and answer any questions. Remind the participant that they can always check the button labels and block hints during the task, so they do not need to memorize them. At this stage, they only need to understand the task correctly. Once the participant understands the task, begin the practice trials and follow the feedback protocol below.

\noindent\textbf{If participant mixes up block types:}
\begin{saybox}
\sayheader
Remember, your reference rule depends on the frame color and hint at top.

\textit{(Point to the frame and hint on the screen.)}

Right now you're comparing to the the rule from the [previous trial / first trial of the block].
%[Yellow/Cyan] and ``Previous'' means compare to the pair you just saw in the previous trial. [Cyan/Yellow] and ``First'' means compare to the very first pair from the start of the block.
\end{saybox}

\noindent\textbf{If participant compares the two pictures within a trial to each other:}
\begin{saybox}
\sayheader
Remember, the top and bottom picture are always two examples of the same rule.
\end{saybox}


\section*{4. Before the scan}

Before bringing the participant into the scanner room:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Confirm that the participant understands the task and has completed the practice trials.

    \item Confirm the participant number and corresponding colour mapping/version.

    \item Make sure the correct participant session files are prepared.

    \item Offer the participant the opportunity to use the bathroom before entering the scanner.
\end{itemize}
